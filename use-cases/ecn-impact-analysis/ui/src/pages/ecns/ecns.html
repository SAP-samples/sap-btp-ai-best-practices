What triggers these generation counters?

In progress

Counts queued jobs waiting to be claimed plus running jobs currently generating a summary. A job enters when queued and stays counted while running; it leaves when generation completes, fails, or is held as Waiting for AI. Waiting for AI (blocked) jobs are not included.

Generation needs attention

Counts failed generation jobs only: a technical failure prevented generation from finishing, even if partial results were saved. Choose the Generation needs attention card, open an ECN with View to inspect its details, and use Retry analysis when appropriate. A job leaves this counter when it is queued again for retry; a retry can fail again.

Ready for summary, completed summaries (including complete with gaps), and Waiting for AI do not contribute to Generation needs attention. Neither counter measures source-data warnings, source release status, manufacturing feasibility, or engineering-review decisions.
